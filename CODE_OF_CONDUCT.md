# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Contributors should communicate professionally, assume good intent, focus on technical facts, and accept feedback aimed at improving the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, and conduct that would make a reasonable participant feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct violates this standard. Report concerns privately through the contact method in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Do not include sensitive vulnerability details in a public issue.

This code applies to project spaces and to conduct that affects participation in them. It is intended to support healthy technical collaboration and does not replace applicable laws or platform rules.
